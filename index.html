The point of the exercise

Build a brand, not an archive.

There is no single correct amount of professional history to share. The useful profile is the one that creates opportunity while keeping you in control of your information.

  • Share ideas.

  • Keep learning.
  • Respect privacy.
  • Let your work speak louder than your timeline.
